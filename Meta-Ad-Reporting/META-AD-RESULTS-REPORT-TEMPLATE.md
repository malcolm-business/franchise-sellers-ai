## Metric glossary (plain-language, reuse these phrasings)
- **Ad spend:** what we paid Meta in total for the run.
- **Reach:** number of individual people who saw the ad at least once.
- **Impressions:** total times the ad appeared on screen (the same person can see it more than once).
- **Frequency:** impressions divided by reach; the average number of times each person saw it. About 1 to 2 is healthy.
- **Clicks to the listing (link clicks):** people who clicked through toward the listing.
- **Overall click-through rate (CTR):** the share of times the ad was shown that led to any click. Around 1 percent is typical for Meta; higher is better.
- **Cost per click (CPC):** what each click cost.
- **Buyer leads (form fills):** people who submitted the inquiry form. A real interest signal; quality is still unknown at this stage.
- **Cost per lead:** spend divided by leads (this matches the "Per Lead" figure on the dashboard card).

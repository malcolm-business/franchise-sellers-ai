# Services, Pricing & Process

The current offer to the marketplace, the two service tiers, pricing, the lead-to-close process, and credibility markers. The offer is identical across both Franchise Sellers and Company Sellers brands — same OOV process, same Toolkit and Full Service tiers, same fees. Only the audience framing differs.

---

## The front-door offer

A **free, complimentary Opinion of Value (OOV)** delivered to qualifying business owners. This is the offer cold email leads with. It's the industry-standard offer for business brokerage, and it's the universal entry point — every prospect who eventually signs an agreement first goes through an OOV.

The OOV is a personalized valuation of the seller's business based on their financial data, industry comparables, addbacks, and market trends. It's delivered via a Loom video walkthrough plus a recap email.

---

## The two service tiers

After the OOV is delivered, qualifying sellers can move forward via one of two paths.

### Full Service

The team manages the entire sale process — discovery call, OOV, listing creation, all buyer conversations, negotiations, and through to closing. The seller's involvement after signing is light: respond to information requests, review the CIM, attend the seller interview, sign documents at key milestones. The team owns the buyer relationship end-to-end.

**Pricing:** 8% commission on the sale, with a $20,000 minimum.

**Positioning vs. market:** Standard business broker commissions are 10–12%. The 8% rate is the "discounted fee structure" referenced in positioning material — roughly two points below market.

### Toolkit

A flat-fee marketing + buyer vetting model. The team handles the marketing infrastructure (listing creation, ad copy, CIM, posting across BizBuySell, BusinessesForSale.com, BusinessBroker.net, DealStream, plus Axial for larger deals), buyer screening and qualification, the data room, monthly analytics, and ongoing Q&A from the Toolkit Manager. The seller handles direct conversations with qualified buyers, negotiations, and the closing process themselves.

**Pricing:**

- $3,500 for the first location
- $2,500 for each additional location
- 6-month marketing agreement
- $750 for a 3-month extension after the initial 6 months
- **No commission** at any point — the entire engagement is the flat fee

**When Toolkit makes sense:** sellers who are comfortable handling buyer conversations themselves, or who have a smaller deal where 8% commission would be punitive relative to the marketing-only cost. Multi-location franchise sellers also benefit since each additional location is an incremental $2,500 vs. paying full commission on each.

### Service mix

Roughly **50/50** split between Toolkit and Full Service among signed agreements. Both tiers are real revenue lines, not one a primary and the other an afterthought.

## The lead-to-close process

This is the standard journey for every signed seller, regardless of which tier they ultimately select.

### 1. Lead Intake

All entry points (referral, direct email, website forms, ads, LinkedIn, events, cold email reply) route to a single Lead Intake Form (Name, Email, Phone, Business Name, basics). New leads route to the Admin Team. Automation triggers SMS/email nudges if no booking occurs.

### 2. Discovery Call

Auto-confirmed via email + SMS, with reminders. Sales team completes a post-call form to advance the opportunity.

### 3. OOV Questionnaire

After the discovery call, the seller receives a DealBuilder intake link with the OOV Questionnaire. The questionnaire covers:

- Owner details (contact info, source attribution)
- Business details (franchisor, business name, address, year started, target listing price, reasons for considering exit)
- Franchise brand specifics (territories, original franchise agreement value)
- Staffing and ownership role (full-time/part-time count, owner role: absentee/semi-absentee/full-time, W-2 salaries)
- Assets and inventory (equipment, vehicles, fixtures, sellable inventory, leasehold improvements)
- Facility and lease information
- Financial addbacks (personal expenses run through the business)
- Document attachments (3 years' financials, YTD P&L, owner W-2, FFE list)

Follow-up cadence if the questionnaire isn't completed: SMS at 5 minutes, email + SMS at Day 4, phone call at Day 8, email + SMS at Day 15.

### 4. Financial Review & OOV Delivery

Once submitted, AI parses the questionnaire for completeness and key metrics (Revenue, SDE, margins, trends, confidence score). If complete and validated, Eric Payne completes the valuation: uploads the recast Excel, records a Loom walkthrough, captures notes. The OOV is delivered via:

- Email with Loom URL
- Confirming SMS
- 3-day follow-up email
- Phone call 2 days later if no response

### 5. Agreement & Payment

When a seller is ready to engage, they're routed to either Toolkit or Full Service. The workflow waits for both: agreement signed AND payment complete. Once both are true, the opportunity moves to Onboarding.

### 6. Listing Creation

The Toolkit Manager (Laurie Martin) or sales team handles onboarding depending on tier. CIM gets built via DealBuilder, ad copy gets drafted (with marketing manager approval), ad goes live across all major business-for-sale platforms, buyer email blast goes out to the existing buyer pool.

### 7. Buyer Flow

Buyers register through franchisesellers.com/buyer-registration, complete an NDA specific to the listing, and a buyer profile (refreshed within 6 months). The Buyer Manager qualifies each one against the listing's financial and background requirements before any introduction. Approved buyers get CIM and Level 1 data room access.

**Data room access is staged:**

- Level 1 (granted on approval): P&L, redacted tax returns, balance sheets, recast worksheet, photos, territory map, video, articles & podcasts, public franchisor materials
- Level 2 (granted after a meaningful seller conversation): franchise agreements, leases, asset list, FFE list

Sensitive documents (unredacted tax returns, listing agreements, internal seller files) live in a Seller-Only section that buyers never access.

### 8. Toolkit-Specific: Seller-Owned Buyer Conversations

For Toolkit sellers, after the introduction email, the seller owns communication with the buyer. The Toolkit Manager remains available behind the scenes for Q&A. There's a next-day "Did you connect?" check-in and a 30-day marketing review.

### 9. Offer & Closing

LOI → Negotiation → Due Diligence → APA → Franchisor Approval → Landlord Approval (if leased space) → Closing. Templated documents are provided in rough draft form; sellers are strongly recommended to use their own attorney for finalization. For Full Service, the team is in the room throughout. For Toolkit, the team supports via Q&A.

### 10. Post-Sale

Seller gift, satisfaction survey, referral ask, content/social posting.

## Differentiators (credibility markers for cold copy)

These are the points to weave into outbound messaging:

**Founder experience.** Eric Payne, founder, has **28 years** of brokerage experience (per Eric's ICP, locked 2026-06-29; supersedes the earlier "27"). This is the lead credibility marker — not brand age. The brands themselves are 5–10 years old, but that's not a number to feature.

**Solution flexibility.** Two real service tiers — Full Service (commission) and Toolkit (flat fee). Most competitors offer only one. The Toolkit option in particular is a differentiator for sellers who don't want commission economics on smaller deals.

**Discounted commission rate.** 8% Full Service vs. 10–12% market standard at competitors like Transworld Brokers, Sunbelt Brokers, National Franchise Sales. This is provable, comparable, and moves the conversation away from price as the deciding factor.

**Nationwide buyer pool.** Active buyer database that gets every new listing emailed on launch + monthly newsletter inclusion. Exact size: not currently quantified — to confirm and add as a specific number when known (a hard number like "X,XXX active buyers in our database" is materially more compelling than "nationwide" in cold copy).

**Deep franchise resale knowledge.** Specialized expertise in franchise-system specifics (FDDs, franchisor approval processes, territory transfers, multi-unit portfolios). Most generalist brokers don't carry this depth.

**Strong OOV → signed agreement conversion.** 20–35% of OOVs delivered convert to a signed Toolkit or Full Service agreement. This is high for the industry and reflects both audience targeting quality and the trust the OOV process builds.

## Target market

**Included:** US business owners across nearly all verticals.

**Excluded:** Professional Services, E-Commerce, Technology. These verticals have specialized M&A advisors better suited to their deal mechanics, so the team passes on them.

**Audience by brand:**
- Franchise Sellers → franchisee owners (operators of franchise system units)
- Company Sellers → independent business owners (non-franchised)

## Competition (for context)

Direct competitors in the space:

- Franchise Flippers
- National Franchise Sales
- Transworld Brokers
- Sunbelt Brokers
- Local Biz Brokers (regional generalists)

The competitive positioning is: more flexible (Toolkit option), lower commission (8% vs. 10–12%), deeper franchise expertise (vs. generalists), nationwide reach (vs. local-only competitors).

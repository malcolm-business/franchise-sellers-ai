## What's Included in Toolkit

The Toolkit service is designed to handle the marketing and buyer qualification side of selling your business, while you stay in the driver's seat for buyer conversations, negotiations, and closing.

**We handle:** Listing creation and ad posting across all major business-for-sale sites, CIM development, buyer screening and qualification, Data Room setup and access management, monthly buyer analytics, and ongoing Q&A support from Laurie throughout the process.

**You handle:** Communicating directly with qualified buyers after they are introduced to you, negotiating terms, coordinating with your attorney and advisors, and managing the closing process.

This is different from the Full Service offering, where the team manages the entire sale process — from buyer conversations through closing — and earns a commission on the sale. With Toolkit, there is no commission. The seller pays a flat fee for the marketing and support described above.

> **Important:** We strongly recommend having an attorney (and ideally an accountant) in place before the first serious buyer conversation. Franchise Sellers provides templated selling documents in rough draft form, but always recommends having an attorney review and finalize any agreements. If a referral is needed, Laurie can point in the right direction.

## 3. Seller Interview Q&A Zoom

Laurie coordinates a Seller Interview Q&A Zoom with the seller as soon as possible. This conversation is an important part of the CIM because it helps tell the story of the business clearly and answer common buyer questions.

If the interview is delayed, the team will not wait — the CIM goes live to qualified buyers and gets updated after the interview is completed. The meeting is scheduled for one hour but is usually completed in 40 minutes or less.

**Typical Seller Interview Topics:**

- What was your background before starting this business?
- Why did you choose this franchise/business?
- Tell us how the business actually runs day-to-day.
- What do your best customers look like, and why do they choose you?
- What has worked best for generating and keeping customers?
- How has your experience been working with the franchisor?
- What have been the biggest challenges in running the business?
- What has made this business successful over time?
- If a new owner came in, where should they focus first to be successful?
- Why are you selling the business now?

## 5. The Buyer Process

**Buyer registration link:** www.franchisesellers.com/buyer-registration

| Step | What Happens |
|------|--------------|
| **Step 1** | An interested buyer is required to complete a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) specific to the listing. |
| **Step 2** | The buyer is required to have an up-to-date (within the last 6 months) buyer profile. |
| **Step 3** | The Buyer Manager qualifies the buyer based on the listing requirements and the buyer's profile submission. |
| **Step 4** | Once approved, the seller receives a buyer inquiry introduction email. This includes the buyer and Laurie, along with the buyer profile submission. The buyer is granted CIM and data room (Level 1) access. |
| **Step 5** | At this point, the seller is responsible for communicating with the buyer and setting up an introductory call. On the first call: cover background and why the business was started, high-level day-to-day operations, the buyer's background and goals, and any initial questions. Conversational tone, not a negotiation. Laurie is available for prep. |

## 6. The Data Room — How Access Works

The data room is a secure document portal inside the Seller Portal. Buyer access is released in stages — each level unlocks additional documents as the buyer moves further in the process. This protects sensitive information until the buyer has demonstrated appropriate interest and engagement.

| Level | When Access Is Granted | Documents Included |
|-------|------------------------|--------------------|
| **Level 1 — Core Business Documents (Redacted)** | When a buyer is first approved by the Buyer Manager | P&L Statements, Redacted Tax Returns, Balance Sheets, Recast Worksheet, Photos, Territory Map, Video, Articles & Podcasts, Public Franchisor Marketing Material |
| **Level 2 — Supplemental Documents** | After a meaningful conversation with the buyer, the seller notifies Laurie and she gives the buyer access to this folder | Franchise Agreements, Leases, Asset List, Furniture/Fixtures/Equipment (FFE) List |

Private documents — including unredacted tax returns, listing agreements, and internal seller files — are stored in a Seller-Only section that buyers can never access.

## 8. When a Buyer Wants to Make an Offer

Typical next steps:

1. **Letter of Intent (LOI)** — buyer submits a letter outlining proposed terms (price, timeline, contingencies). Rough draft LOI template provided.
2. **Negotiation** — seller and buyer (often with respective attorneys) negotiate final terms. Laurie available for Q&A throughout.
3. **Due Diligence** — once terms are agreed, the buyer reviews additional financial and operational documents to verify business details. Normal and expected.
4. **Asset Purchase Agreement (APA)** — formal purchase agreement drafted and signed by both parties. Rough draft APA template provided. Strongly recommend attorney review.
5. **Franchisor Approval** — buyer must be approved by the franchisor before sale can be finalized. Buyer typically submits an application directly to the franchisor and completes any required interviews or training. Timelines vary by brand — start the process as early as possible.
6. **Landlord Approval (if applicable)** — if the business operates from a leased location, the landlord must approve the lease assignment or enter into a new lease with the buyer. Coordinated between attorneys and landlord.
7. **Closing** — transaction completed, typically coordinated between attorneys, ownership transfers to the buyer.

Throughout this process, Laurie and the team are available behind the scenes for Q&A.

## 9. Monthly Buyer Analytics

A monthly summary of buyer activity and listing performance is provided. Includes listing views, buyer inquiries, and other key activity metrics from business-for-sale sites where the listing is posted.

> **Important:** Every time the CIM presentation PDF is re-uploaded to the system, tracking codes reset and CIM view stats return to zero.

At the end of each month, Laurie needs to know which buyers are no longer interested in the listing so they can be marked lost and have access revoked.

---

## 10. Ongoing Document Uploads

Monthly updated financials (YTD P&L) are requested, ideally by the 15th of each month. Buyers expect to see current numbers — outdated financials slow deals and erode buyer confidence.

Monthly documents can be emailed to Laurie or uploaded through the Seller Portal. Secure upload link available on request.

Examples: monthly financials, updated rent or royalty notices, revised reports, asset list updates, facility or lease documents, photos, videos, or other buyer support materials.

## 12. Marketing Term and Extensions

The Toolkit program typically includes a **6-month marketing agreement**.

After the first 6 months, if the seller would like to continue marketing the business, the standard marketing extension is **$750 for a 3-month term**. This covers continued ad placement across all business-for-sale sites, buyer qualification, CIM and Data Room access management, and ongoing Q&A support from Laurie.

This extension amount may vary for multi-location listings, as those often require multiple campaigns or additional campaign management.

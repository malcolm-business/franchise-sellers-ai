# Toolkit Manager SOP — Laurie

> Source: Notion page "Toolkit Manager SOP — Laurie" under Operations & SOP Library > Toolkit Service. Last updated April 2026.
> Owner: Laurie (Toolkit Manager). Applies to: Franchise Sellers & Company Sellers.

## Purpose

This is the day-to-day operational SOP for the Toolkit Manager. The Toolkit service is a marketing + buyer vetting model — the team markets the business, qualifies buyers, and hands qualified leads to the seller. **The seller owns the buyer relationship after handoff.** Laurie supports the seller throughout and manages the listing lifecycle.

> For Deal Studio automation specs and system workflows, see Toolkit SOP — Deal Studio (`05-toolkit-sop-deal-studio.md`).

## 1. Seller Onboarding

**Trigger:** Agreement signed + payment received. Deal Studio creates the listing and notifies Laurie.

**Laurie's steps:**

1. Confirm the Listing Onboarding Questionnaire has been sent to the seller (this is triggered in the welcome email)
2. Reach out to the seller to schedule the **Seller Interview Q&A Zoom** as soon as possible
3. Provide the seller with the Toolkit Process Guide (Client Handout), which includes:
   - Their seller portal link
   - Explanation of the CIM, data room, and questionnaire
   - How the buyer process works
   - Marketing terms and extensions info
4. Confirm whether the listing will be **branded** or **confidential**
5. Coordinate with the Admin Team / Marketing Manager on ad copy creation and approval

**Key timing:** Market the business live ASAP — typically within 3 days of listing. If the seller interview is delayed, do **not** wait. The CIM goes live to qualified buyers, and gets updated after the interview is completed.

## 2. Seller Interview & Video

1. Seller books the interview via Laurie's calendar link
2. Laurie fills the short Interview Prep Form (stored on the Opportunity in Deal Studio)
3. After the Zoom call:
   - Recording is emailed to Rich automatically
   - Rich has a task to edit the video (7-day reminder fires if not completed)
   - Rich uploads the final video; DealBuilder embeds it into the Business Summary (CIM)
4. Once the video is ready, update the CIM and data room
5. Send the updated CIM to the seller for review

> The meeting is usually scheduled for 1 hour but often completed in 30 minutes or less.

## 3. Ad Copy & Listing Approval

1. Admin Team drafts ad copy (AI assist allowed)
2. Marketing Manager receives an approval email with trigger links:
   - ✅ **Approve to Publish** → goes live
   - ❌ **Needs Revision** → returns to Admin Team; new approval sent on resubmission
3. Once Marketing Manager approves, the seller receives a portal invite to view/approve the ad
4. Laurie oversees the timeline and handles seller questions (does not own final ad approval)

**Branded vs. Confidential:**

- **Confidential listings:** Brand name hidden; shows City, County, State (city can be excluded on request)
- **Branded listings:** Shows Brand Name, City, County, State
- Recommend starting branded, but the decision is the seller's
- One free switch between branded and confidential

## 4. Buyer Handoff (From Yvonne to Seller)

**Context:** Yvonne (Buyer Manager) handles all buyer qualification — NDA, buyer profile, and vetting against listing requirements. Once a buyer is qualified, Yvonne hands off to Laurie and the seller.

**Handoff steps (performed by Yvonne):**

1. Yvonne sends the **Buyer Inquiry Introduction Email** to the seller, cc'ing Laurie
2. Email includes the buyer profile submission and NDA confirmation
3. Yvonne grants the buyer **Level 1 access** to the buyer portal (CIM + Data Room Level 1)

**From this point, the seller owns communication with the buyer.**

**Laurie's responsibilities after handoff:**

1. Check in with the seller shortly after to confirm they received and connected with the buyer
2. If the seller reports the buyer is interested and wants deeper materials → **Laurie grants Level 2 Data Room access** (auto-includes Level 1)
3. If the seller wants a buyer's access revoked → Laurie removes access in DealBuilder
4. By default, buyers **cannot download** documents from the data room. If the seller requests download access for a specific buyer, Laurie enables it.

> **Remember:** Toolkit = the team markets and vets buyers and delivers qualified leads. The seller handles the buyer relationship from there. Laurie supports but does not manage the buyer-seller conversation.

## 5. Seller Portal & Data Room Management

- Sellers can view the ad, CIM/Business Summary, and upload documents via the DealBuilder seller portal
- Every seller upload creates an internal review task for Laurie (publication paused until approved)
- **Data Room Release:** Default is FALSE. When set to TRUE, automation sends Data Room access to qualified buyers.

**Portal login troubleshooting (buyers and sellers):**

- **Cookies:** Clear browser cookies and retry
- **Email address:** Must use the exact email registered in DealBuilder
- **Stale view:** Refresh the screen to see updates (new buyers, CIM changes)

## 6. Marketing Channels & Coordination

| Channel | What Happens |
|---------|--------------|
| **Business listing sites** | Admin Team creates ads; sent to seller for approval before going live. Reviewed periodically for performance. |
| **Initial buyer email blast** | Once CIM is completed, a targeted blast goes to the buyer network. |
| **Monthly buyer newsletter** | Listing included in the monthly newsletter to buyers, when applicable. |
| **Buyer blasts** | Reference the Buyer Blasts SOP for timing and process. Laurie gathers and reports stats after blasts. |

**Ongoing marketing tasks:**

- Confirm monthly that listings are live and accurate on all advertising sites
- Check expiration dates on listings and flag upcoming expirations
- Share DealBuilder analytics screenshot with seller monthly

> **Important:** Every time the CIM presentation PDF is re-uploaded, tracking codes reset and CIM view stats return to zero. Document the reset date each time.

## 7. Monthly Listing Maintenance

Laurie runs a monthly check-in cycle for every active Toolkit listing:

1. **Seller check-in:** Email/call to confirm ad accuracy, any updates, price changes, and selling intent
2. **Financial updates:** Request monthly updated financials (YTD P&L) from the seller. Savvy buyers want current data alongside historical.
3. **Upload handling:** Seller emails documents to Laurie or uses the secure upload link (provided on request)
4. **Ad refresh:** If any changes are needed, ping Admin Team and Marketing Manager for action/approval
5. **CIM updates:** Track when the CIM is updated; note the analytics reset date

**Examples of documents sellers may send:** monthly financials, updated rent or royalty notices, revised reports, asset list updates, facility or lease documents, photos, videos, or other buyer support materials.

## 8. Marketing Term & Extensions

- Toolkit program includes a **6-month marketing agreement**
- After the first 6 months: standard extension is **$750 for a 3-month term**
- Multi-location listings may vary (often require multiple campaigns or additional management)

## 9. Reporting & Oversight

- Central dashboards filter Toolkit vs. Full Service via Listing Type
- Weekly exec summary and monthly management report generated automatically
- Reports include: active listings, buyer intros, ad health
- Monthly buyer analytics summary provided to each seller

## 10. Documents & Agreements

- For selling documents and agreements, provide the seller with the Dropbox link (included in the Client Handout)
- **Important:** Sellers should work closely with their attorney, accountant, and other advisors. Franchise Sellers provides templated documents only and always recommends using an attorney at minimum for closing procedures.

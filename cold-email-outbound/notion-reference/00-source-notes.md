## Discrepancies and notes for Theodore to confirm

A few things in the Notion source material conflict with what Theodore stated verbally on 2026-05-03. Worth resolving before any copy goes out:

**1. Full Service pricing.** The Reply Handling SOP (`07-reply-handling-sop.md`) lists Full Service as "$1,000 onboarding fee + 8% success commission on the final sale price." Theodore stated current pricing is "8% and minimum is $20K" — no mention of a $1,000 onboarding fee. **Action:** confirm whether the $1,000 onboarding fee still applies, and whether the $20K minimum has replaced it or is additive. The synthesized doc (`06-services-and-process.md`) currently uses Theodore's verbal answer (8% / $20K minimum, no onboarding fee mentioned).

**2. Toolkit pricing.** The Reply Handling SOP lists Toolkit as "$3,500 onboarding fee + ZERO commission on the sale" with no per-location pricing detail. Theodore stated current pricing is "$3,500 first location, $2,500 each additional location, no commission, $750 for 3-month extensions." **Action:** confirm the Reply SOP needs updating to reflect the multi-location pricing tier. The synthesized doc uses Theodore's version.

**3. Brand age framing.** The Zor-Prospecting Framework (`09-zor-prospecting-framework.md`) says "Franchise Sellers has been specializing in resales for over 25 years." Theodore confirmed: brands are 5–10 years old, but founder Eric Payne has 27 years of brokerage experience. The Zor framing implicitly credits Eric's experience to the brand. Theodore's guidance: don't mention brand age in cold copy — lean on Eric's experience as the credibility marker. **Action:** the existing Zor cold call script is fine because it's spoken language, but cold email copy should follow Theodore's guidance and credit experience to "our broker president" rather than the brand.

**4. Special pricing for partnered franchise systems.** The Reply Handling SOP references special pricing for Xponential / FitnessTogether franchisees due to a relationship with the franchisor. **Action:** if Xponential or related-system franchisees end up in a cold campaign, ensure the cold email copy doesn't conflict with the special-pricing offer. May warrant a separate spintax variant or an exclusion segment.

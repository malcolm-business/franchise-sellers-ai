# FS-Opinion of Value (OOV) Questionnaire — Reference

> Source: Notion page "FS-Opinion of Value (OOV) Questionnaire — Reference" under Operations & SOP Library > Onboarding & Listings. Live version is managed in Deal Studio (DealBuilder); this is a reference copy.

## Purpose

This is a reference copy of the OOV Questionnaire used in Deal Studio (DealBuilder). It is sent to sellers **before** the valuation (Opinion of Value) to gather the key information needed to assess the business. **Do not edit here** — the live version is managed in Deal Studio.

## Description

The OOV Questionnaire is designed to gather key information about the business to support an accurate valuation and a smooth sales process. It covers essential details including ownership, business operations, franchise brand specifics, staffing, assets, facilities, financial addbacks, and additional insights. Sellers also upload financial documents to ensure a complete and thorough review.

---

## 1. Owner Details

- Owner Name
- Other owners & shareholders
- Best Phone
- Owner Email
- How Did You Hear About Franchise Sellers?
  - Referred by the Franchisor
  - Referred by a Franchise/Business Owner
  - Referred by a Business Advisor
  - Google or Social Media
  - Event or Conference
  - Contacted Directly by Our Team

---

## 2. Business Details

- Name of the Franchisor
- Corporation/Company Name
- Business Name (Doing Business As)
- Business Website
- Business Address
- Year Started
- Briefly describe what the business/franchise does
- Every business is unique — provide any information not elsewhere provided to help us understand what makes this business different from others
- Target Listing Price
- What are the main reasons you are considering a business exit?

---

## 3. Franchise Brand Details

- How many territories are included? Do you have additional territories to sell? Are they open or unused? Will they be included or sold separately?
- What was the original value of your franchise agreement(s)?

---

## 4. Staffing & Ownership Role

- Provide the total number of full-time employees
- Provide the total number of part-time employees
- What is your role as the owner? *(Required)*
  - Absentee (Less than 5 hours/week)
  - Semi-Absentee (Part-time)
  - Full-Time
- Do you take a W-2 salary (not distributions)? If yes, what is your annual W-2 salary?
- Whether the company is or is not run absentee, if this company WERE to be run absentee, what would an absentee owner have to pay a general manager annually, including all benefits and bonuses, to run this business profitably?
- List all family members, relatives and/or partners, INCLUDING THE OWNER, that are actively working in this business, as well as the W-2 salary for each
- If the owner left or passed away unexpectedly, how would profits and revenue be impacted?
- Are all owners and employees making Fair Market Wages? If not, what adjustments need to be made and to whom?

---

## 5. Assets & Inventory

- What is the current market value of OWNED equipment, vehicles, and fixtures?
- What is the average value of Sellable Inventory (at cost) on hand?
- What is the average daily value of stocks, supplies or merchandise for internal consumption?
- What is the current market value of your leasehold improvements?

---

## 6. Facility & Lease Information

- What best describes your business location?
  - Owned
  - Leased
  - Home-Based
- Tell us more about the facility (e.g. large corner unit, freestanding building, new buildout, needs updating, highway exposure, etc.) — the more information the better
- Facility square footage

---

## 7. Financial Addbacks

- What personal expenses are being run through the business? (Examples: personal auto, meals & entertainment, travel, contributions, medical insurance)
- What is the annualized value of all personal addbacks?

---

## 8. Attachments

- Last 3 years' financial statements (and/or) Tax Returns
- Year-to-date Profit & Loss Statement (Jan 1 through last completed month)
- Owner's W-2 salary each year plus current year. If owner is absentee, then highest paid manager's salary info
- List of Furniture, Fixtures, and Equipment (if applicable)

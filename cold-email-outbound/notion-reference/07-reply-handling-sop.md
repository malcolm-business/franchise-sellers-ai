## Purpose

This SOP keeps track of appropriate replies for Instantly email campaigns, ensuring the CRM stays clean and organized. Covers both **Franchise Sellers (FS)** and **Company Sellers (CS)** campaigns.

## General Rules

- After any reply action, **update the label status** in Instantly to the appropriate value
- Always use the **correct brand signature** (FS or CS) based on which campaign the reply came from
- For Business Advisors replies: always use Theodore's Calendly (`https://calendly.com/theodorebaird`) — do NOT copy support

---

## Reply Scenarios — FS Campaigns

### Client Has Sold Their Business

**If it's a non-automated reply:** Send acknowledgment, then update label to "No longer with company/Sold". Paste the name and email in the Airtable Slack channel and notify Theodore to update the client info.

If new owner info was provided, share it with Theodore as well.

> Hi [Name],
>
> Thanks for letting us know—and congratulations on the sale! If you ever find yourself exploring new ventures, need support with a future exit, or know someone else who could benefit from our services, we're always here to help.
>
> Wishing you all the best in your next chapter!
>
> Theodore Baird | Franchise Sellers | Direct: 720-679-8300 | www.franchisesellers.com

**If it's an automated reply** (e.g., "Dear Sender"): No response needed.

### Marked as Interested / Calendly Link Sent

Ensure to **BCC: support@franchisesellers.com** so the lead can be created in the CRM.

### Stop / Unsubscribe / Stop Spamming

Do NOT reply. Update label to **"Unsubscribed — DNC"** then mark as done.

### Auto-Replies / Out of Office

No status change needed. Just mark as **done**.

### Email Has Changed

Update label to **"Invalid Email"** then mark as done.

## Reply Scenarios — CS Campaigns

### Selling Several Years Out

> Totally understand that exiting isn't something you're focused on right now. That makes complete sense.
>
> Typical business sale preparation can take 6 months to 2 years. With that in mind, we're always happy to offer a complimentary business valuation—no obligation—just to give you a sense of what your business might be worth today.
>
> We can also walk you through general steps for preparing a business for sale, even if it's just to get educated for the future. Happy to follow your lead—whether checking back in 6 months or setting something up sooner.

### Selling 10 Years Out

> Thanks for getting back to me!
>
> In my experience, the most successful exits are the ones planned years in advance. You can start making intentional decisions today that will have a massive impact on your business's value when you're ready to sell.
>
> Knowing your current valuation provides a clear benchmark. It helps identify key value drivers and areas to focus on for growth. We keep confidentiality at the highest level and would love to have a discussion.

### Franchise Launching Inquiry

> Thanks for sharing—sounds like you've put in the hard work and built something amazing. Just to clarify, our sister brand, Franchise Sellers, specializes in helping franchise business owners value and **sell** their existing businesses.
>
> We don't assist with turning private businesses into franchise systems—but we partner with a great resource for that: **Adam Valdez**. He works directly with entrepreneurs looking to franchise their concept.
>
> If you're more interested in selling your current business, we'd be happy to offer a free, no-obligation valuation. If your focus is launching the franchise side, just let me know and I'll make an email introduction to Adam.

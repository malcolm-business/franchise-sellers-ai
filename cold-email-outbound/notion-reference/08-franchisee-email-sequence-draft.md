## Notes for the rebuilt engine

These templates date from a previous era of cold email — pre-2026 best practices. Worth noting what works and what to leave behind:

**What carries forward:** the OOV as the front-door offer, the dual-tier framing (Full Service + Toolkit), confidentiality as a trust marker, and the multi-touch cadence concept.

**What to update for 2026:** length (these emails are 200+ words; 2026 best practice is 50–125 words, ideally under 70 per Ben Reed). Personalization (these are generic; 2026 reply rates require signal-anchored opens referencing something specific to the prospect). Subject lines (current ones are statement-style; "quick question about [company]" patterns outperform). CTA framing (current ones say "reply or call" which is heavy; soft questions like "worth a quick conversation?" outperform).

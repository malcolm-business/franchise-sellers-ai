# High-Level Blueprint

> Source: Notion page "High-Level Blueprint" under Franchise Sellers > Strategy & Brand. Last modified 2026-04-06.

## Business Model Overview

Franchise Sellers operates two interconnected sides:

1. **Creating New Listings** — generating and converting seller leads into active listings
2. **Selling Those Listings** — finding qualified buyers and facilitating the transaction to close

Each side has two core components: marketing/lead generation and sales/conversion.

---

## Lead Generation

**Our Offer**

Nationwide business brokerage with a streamlined, full-service and DIY solution. We help business owners value, market, and sell their businesses quickly and confidentially. Core offer includes a free business valuation, expert brokerage support, and a proven process from initial consultation through closing — maximizing value and minimizing stress.

**Key Differentiators:** Solution flexibility (Full Service or Toolkit), nationwide buyer pool, discounted fee structure, deep understanding of the franchise resale process.

**Competition:** Franchise Flippers, National Franchise Sales, Transworld Brokers, Sunbelt Brokers, Local Biz Brokers

**Target Market:** US Business Owners
*Exclude: Professional Services, E-Commerce, Technology*

**Data Sources**

- Buy data via Clay
- Google Maps scraping

**How We Reach Them**

*Inbound:*

- Referrals — Franchisors, Business Advisors, Franchise Consultants
- Events & Organizations
- Social Media & Content

*Outbound:*

- Email Campaigns
- LinkedIn Campaigns
- Google & Social Ads
- Direct Mail
- Broker Listing Sites

**Lead Gen Tools:** Funnels, landing pages, webinars, calendar routing forms, lead magnets (white papers, PDF guides)

## Lead Conversion

**Definition:** Conversion begins at the Discovery Call and ends after the Opinion of Value (OOV) is delivered.

1. Discovery Call Framework
2. OOV — Financial Document Request
3. OOV Report & Presentation

---

## Lead Nurturing

**Definition:** After the OOV, when a lead either doesn't hire us yet or asks us to stay in touch.

1. Email Nurturing Campaign
2. Phone / FaceTime Check-Ins
3. LinkedIn Connection
4. Online Ad Retargeting Campaign

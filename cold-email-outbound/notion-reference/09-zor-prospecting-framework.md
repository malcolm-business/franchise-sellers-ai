> "Zor" = Franchisor. This framework is for prospecting franchise system corporate teams as referral partners — they refer their franchisees who want to sell to Franchise Sellers.

## Reference for the sales team when prospecting new franchisor referral partners.

---

## Discovery Call / Presentation Guide

Use this structure when a call is booked with a franchise development or operations contact.

1. **Open with a diagnostic question:** How do you currently handle resales? What's working, what isn't? What's your vision for resales going forward?
2. **Frame the goal:** Help make resales as simple as possible for you and your team — whether fully outsourcing the resale department or supplementing the existing process.
3. **Key value points to hit:**
   - No cost to you. No obligation. No exclusive agreement.
   - When a franchisee reaches out about selling, simply refer them to us.
   - We currently work with dozens of brands across many industries — from 25-location systems to franchise groups with thousands of units.
4. **Walk through the intro email template** — show how easy the referral process is.
5. **Mention value-add resources:** Business valuation webinar/seminar for their franchisees (not a sales pitch — how to prepare and maximize exit value). Brief video explaining the process. Flyers.
6. **National conferences** — ask if they attend any; explore co-marketing.
7. **Client managers can sit in on Discovery Days** and study the FDD to get brand-specific.
8. **Once a listing comes in — confirm logistics:**
   - Who is the franchisor point of contact?
   - When do you want to be looped in — before or after an LOI?
   - Buyer requirements (absentee owners, new vs. existing, etc.)
   - Selling fees for their franchisees.
9. **Corporate locations:** Mention the team can also help sell corporate-owned locations they're looking to offload.

## Our Story (Positioning)

Franchisors need a reliable resource to help manage resales. We take the burden and workload off their shoulders. Partnering with FS is a great option because there is no expense and no obligation to the franchisor — simply refer franchisees to us and we manage the rest, bringing the franchisor in at whatever stage they want.

**Key differentiators:**

- **One point of contact** — no more re-explaining your brand, systems, and preferences to a different local broker every time
- **Outsourced resale department** — we can mirror your branding, understand your processes, sit in on Discovery Days
- **Free 3rd-party valuation** — removes liability from the franchisor; good cop/bad cop dynamic
- **Two resale routes** — Full Service and Toolkit (for more DIY franchisees)
- **Nationwide buyer network** — we always have qualified buyers looking for existing franchise locations

## Cold Call Script

**Opening (Gatekeeper):**

> "Hey, it's Theodore with Franchise Sellers. I'm hoping to connect with the person that handles franchise resales or transfers — is that yourself or somebody else?"

**Opening (Decision Maker):**

> "Hey, it's Theodore with Franchise Sellers. I have a quick question about your franchise resales — did I catch you in the middle of anything? I can be brief or call you back at a better time."

**Qualifying question:**

> "When a franchisee reaches out for advice or guidance about selling their business, do you take those calls yourself — or is that somebody else on your team?"

**Reason for the call:**

> "The reason I'm calling — Franchise Sellers is a nationwide business brokerage, and we help franchisors manage the workload involved with running a successful resale program. We also help remove liability from the franchisor by providing franchisees with a third-party valuation. Do you mind if I ask a bit about your current resale process?"

**If they have a process / open to hearing more:**

> "It sounds like we might be a great resource for you and your team. Do you mind if I take a minute to explain our services?"

**If they don't think they need it:**

> "Understood — it doesn't quite sound like we're the right fit right now. We typically work best with franchisors who don't want to spend too much time on resales but want a predictable, reliable resource when a franchisee is ready to sell or just wants a valuation. I'll circle back in six months and see if anything's changed."

## Who We Are / What Makes Us Different (Pitch Block)

Franchise Sellers has been specializing in resales for over 25 years. We're a nationwide franchise resale and valuation firm.

> ⚠️ **Note on framing:** Theodore confirmed that the brands themselves are 5–10 years old; founder Eric Payne has 27 years of brokerage experience. The "25 years specializing in resales" framing implicitly credits Eric's experience to the brand. Acceptable in spoken sales context but should be more carefully framed in cold email copy ("our broker president has 27 years..." rather than implying brand age).

Franchisors approach resales in many different ways — we're not the right fit for everyone. But we're a great resource for development and operations teams that don't want to spend too much time or energy on resales, but want a predictable, reliable resource when a franchisee is ready to sell or just needs a valuation.

Our goal is to make resales as simple as possible for you and your team.

When your franchisees come to you about selling, our hope is to earn the right to be your first recommendation. We work directly with them and bring you in at whatever stage you want. No exclusives, no agreements — we're paid by the franchisee.

Because we operate nationwide, your team has one point of contact. No more explaining your process and brand specifics to a different local broker each time.

If there's an opportunity here, we'd love to schedule a time to connect with our broker president, Eric Payne. He can answer any specifics and walk through the resale process in detail.

## Why this is relevant to the cold email rebuild

Franchisor referral relationships are one of the highest-leverage referral types specifically for Franchise Sellers (the brand). Each franchise system that refers becomes a steady source of seller leads — every franchisee in their system who eventually wants to exit becomes a referral.

This framework should inform the franchisor outreach motion described in the Notion "Referral Program" page (subpage 6). The cold call script above is the gold-standard for spoken outreach; the cold *email* version of this should be a separate sequence in the Referral Program track of the cold email engine, with the same positioning ("no cost, no obligation, no exclusive") but adapted for inbox consumption.

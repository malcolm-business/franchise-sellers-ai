Non-negotiables for the copy
  • Email 1 starts a conversation, never sells. Under 80 words.
  • One soft CTA (a question). No link in email 1.
  • Credibility, not claims. Use our 27 years, confidentiality, our process. Never "sold for more than they expected."
  • Name confidentiality early. It is the owner's first fear.
  • Frame the free valuation honestly: a no-obligation opinion of value, not a formal appraisal, no guaranteed number.
  • No em dashes, no filler, honest subjects. Specific and exit-themed beats generic.
  • 3 emails, days 1, 4, 6, each shorter than the last.
  • Soft seller breakup at the end. Never "point me to the right person."

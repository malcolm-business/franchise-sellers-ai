## Watch for (per playbook)
- Owner-gated private groups ("Are you a CA business owner?") → skip.
- Buy/sell/trade + generic advertising dump groups → skip.
- Niche identity groups (e.g. Black Business Owners Network 35K) → only when a specific seller/listing fits.
- Posting-gates (participation questions) → join fine, handle honestly at first-post time.

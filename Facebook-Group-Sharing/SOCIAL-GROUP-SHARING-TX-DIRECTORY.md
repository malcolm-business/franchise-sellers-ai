# Facebook Group Sharing — Texas Directory

Master menu of Facebook groups for sharing business listings to reach entrepreneurial / business-buyer audiences in Texas.
Joining + posting done as the **Franchise Sellers Page**. Per-listing, pick the best-fitting groups we're already in. Weekly task drives the posting.

**Group URLs:** direct links to every Texas group here are in `SOCIAL-GROUP-SHARING-MASTER-LINKS.md` (same folder).

## DAILY JOIN PROCESS (Texas, ongoing)
Goal: steadily grow the joined-group base, targeting audiences likely to care about a business-for-sale (owners, entrepreneurs, buyers, investors).
- **Pace guardrail:** Facebook throttles at ~30 join requests / 12 hrs (risk of temporary "action blocked"). Keep each day to **~20 max**. Space them out.
- **Join AS the Franchise Sellers Page.** Skip owner-gated private groups ("Are you a [TX] business owner?") — can't pass honestly as a brokerage.
- **Prefer:** public + active (20+ posts/day) + promo/business-friendly purpose. Log posting-gates.
- **Mechanics note:** join from search results; the FIRST click on a fresh target sometimes misses — verify the button flips to "Visit"/"Joined" and re-click if not.
- **Verify** the day's joins via facebook.com/groups/joins/ ("last visited: a few seconds ago" = joined today).

## KEY FINDINGS
1. Our Page joins big PUBLIC promo/networking groups instantly. That's the lane.
2. Owner-gated PRIVATE groups ("Are you a [TX] business owner?") — skip (can't pass honestly as a brokerage).
3. Joining =/= posting: some groups need a one-time admin-reviewed participation request + questions before first post, and decline promo/spam. Handle per-group at first-post time; posts must read as value, not an ad.
4. "By owner / no broker" groups = poor fit for brokered listings, but full of owners wanting to SELL = potential SELLER leads (the #1 goal). Possible separate seller-outreach play.

## How this powers the weekly SHARE task
Process rules live in **`SOCIAL-GROUP-SHARING-PLAYBOOK.md`** (source of truth). In short: once a week, per listing, we match it to the best-fitting joined groups here (by `[industry] + [metro]`) and draft a **unique, group-tailored message for each group**, packaged into a **weekly email to Rosie** (she posts). Claude drafts the email; Malcolm sends. Gated groups need their participation request completed before first post.
